# Expanded pilot 05 — Risk history and trends

Deliver TASK-031 on the current native pilot. Store actual organization and endpoint scores with scoring context, explain observed changes between captures, and show the retained points in Desktop and exported reports.

- Capture the existing deterministic fleet risk evaluation once per six-hour UTC bucket, at its actual evaluation time. Use one consistent read of active enrollment, observation and alert state, followed by an atomic write. Skip missed buckets, duplicate captures, and backwards clock buckets. Keep organization scoring aligned with the current maximum endpoint method, including the uncapped maximum.
- Retain up to 90 days and at most 250,000 endpoint points. Remove oldest whole captures if the row cap is reached. Bound retained policy and baseline history. Preserve recorded policy version and a canonical SHA-256 identity over effective scoring parameters and algorithm revision. No historical recalculation on policy changes.
- Record bounded typed evidence of changes since the previous recorded measurement, including policy, endpoint context, alert membership, status, severity, observation, aging, confirmation and correlation. Report potentially contributing changes without asserting proof of remediation. Keep top scoring rule IDs and use an unexplained marker when inputs do not justify a narrower explanation. Preserve the comparison timestamp.
- Local administrator-only, no-store and bounded history for organization and endpoint scopes. Support 24 hours and 7/30/90 days. Removed endpoints retain existing points under the same retention rules. Reads do not create measurements.
- In native Risk, display retained samples with accessible points and table, selected period, score and raw score, policy and observations. Do not bridge missing captures. Support PL/EN and 640×480. HTML reports show retained organization measurements within the selected UTC report period separately from the current score. Old backups initialize with empty history and new backups preserve the attached history.
- Exercise scheduling, retries, restart, cap and retention, clock boundaries, score parity, policy/factor changes, scope and permissions, safe report output, session isolation and native views. Record validation in STATUS.
